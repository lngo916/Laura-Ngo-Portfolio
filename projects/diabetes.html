02 · Research questions

What we wanted to know

  1. Which demographics are more likely to have diabetes?
  2. Is BMI a good indicator of an individual's likelihood of developing diabetes?
  3. How do BMI and personal background (demographics) jointly impact diabetes prediction?
Hypothesis
We expect a significant association between BMI and diabetes, with higher BMI values indicating increased diabetes risk.
