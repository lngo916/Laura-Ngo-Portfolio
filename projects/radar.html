MLBB Hero Radar Chart

Compare the attributes of up to five Mobile Legends: Bang Bang heroes using an interactive radar chart.

Hover over a hero on the radar chart or legend to view detailed statistics.

How to use the radar chart
  • Use the dropdowns above to select up to five heroes.
  • Multiple heroes can be compared at the same time.
  • Hover over a radar polygon or legend item to highlight a hero.
  • Hovering over a hero displays its detailed attributes, win rate, and release year.
  • Click Reset All to clear the comparison.
